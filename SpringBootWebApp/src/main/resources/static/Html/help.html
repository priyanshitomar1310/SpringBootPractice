	Welcome to the Help & Support section. Here you can find useful information and guidance to help you use our application effectively.

	**How can we help you?**

	If you are facing any difficulty while using the application, please check the following options:

	* **Login Issues:** Make sure you have entered the correct email/username and password. If you still cannot log in, try resetting your password.
	* **Account Issues:** If you are unable to access or update your account, please contact our support team.
	* **Technical Issues:** If a page is not loading properly or a feature is not working, try refreshing the page or checking your internet connection.
	* **Need More Help?** If your issue is not resolved, please contact our support team with a brief description of the problem.

	**Contact Support**

	For further assistance, please reach out to our support team. We will be happy to help you and resolve your queries as soon as possible.

	Thank you for using our application!
